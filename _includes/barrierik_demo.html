A planar toy with the structure of the paper's pipeline, not its implementation: your target and the autonomous target (the block) are blended linearly, with a sigmoid of their distance as the weight, and an inverse-kinematics step turns the result into joint motion. Baseline N ignores obstacles, Baseline P adds a proximity penalty to the objective, and BarrierIK adds control barrier function constraints ∇h·Δθ + γh ≥ 0 for every link–obstacle pair, so it never moves deeper into the safety margin. Each step is a small quadratic program solved in your browser (Hildreth's method) instead of the paper's SLSQP solver; the numbers come from this toy, not from the paper.
