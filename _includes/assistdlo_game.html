A browser game inspired by the study task. The rope is a small simulation with gravity, table friction and self-collision, so strands cannot pass through each other. As in the study, the knot counts as untied when the rope no longer crosses itself. SA-CBF is shown in simplified form: a barrier keeps the gripper above the rope except in funnels (blue) around rope points, so a click near the rope is guided onto the nearest point, the upper strand at a crossing, and a click away from the rope does not make the gripper descend.
