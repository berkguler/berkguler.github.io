A browser game inspired by the study task and its four conditions, not a reproduction of the study. The rope is a small simulation with gravity, table friction and self-collision, so strands cannot pass through each other. The knot counts as untied once lifting both ends and pulling them apart would leave a straight rope (the study asked for no crossings at all; the game shows the crossing count too). Teleoperation error is modelled as a slowly drifting offset between where you click and where the gripper lands; in the real system it comes from judging depth through a camera image. Your scores stay in this browser tab.
