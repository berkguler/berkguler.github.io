A browser game inspired by the study task. The rope is a small simulation with gravity, table friction, bending stiffness and self-collision, so strands cannot pass through each other. As in the study, the knot counts as untied when the rope no longer crosses itself. Besides the overhand knot you can pick prime knots from the Rolfsen knot table, drawn as closed braids and cut open on the outer strand like an overhand knot, so the rope copies the knot's diagram (an open rope can always be undone). SA-CBF is shown in simplified form: a barrier keeps the gripper above the rope except in funnels (blue) around rope points, so a click near the rope is guided onto the nearest point, the upper strand at a crossing, and a click away from the rope does not make the gripper descend.

Want more? The Knot Lab deals a new shuffled knot every round and keeps a leaderboard.
