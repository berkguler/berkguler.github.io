An illustrative re-implementation of the thesis simulation's admittance model (40 kg virtual mass, 100 N·s/m damping, 125 Hz, the identified velocity loop of the real robot, and GH between 0.33 and 2.68). Two parts are simplified: in Adaptive mode the operator's intent is read from the commanded acceleration (GH·Fh − b·v: pushing harder than needed to hold the current speed means speeding up) instead of the thesis's neural network, and the simulated human follows a minimum-jerk plan instead of the study's learned human model. The numbers shown come from this toy, not from the study.
