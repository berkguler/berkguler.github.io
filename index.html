About me

I am a PhD candidate at the Intelligent Autonomous Systems Lab at TU Darmstadt, advised by Prof. Jan Peters, and I work jointly with Dr. Simon Manschitz at Honda Research Institute Europe. I build assistive teleoperation systems for dexterous manipulation: the robot takes care of safety and precision, and the human stays in charge of the task.

My research spans safety-aware teleoperation algorithms, intention estimation and assistance for manipulating deformable linear objects such as ropes and cables, and the perception and dynamics models these systems build on. Before my PhD, I worked on physical human–robot interaction at Koç University and studied control and automation engineering at Istanbul Technical University.

  • Assistive Teleoperation
  • Deformable Linear Objects
  • DLO Perception
  • Physical Human–Robot Interaction
  • Robot Learning
