About me

I am a PhD candidate at the Intelligent Autonomous Systems Lab at TU Darmstadt, advised by Prof. Jan Peters, and I work jointly with Dr. Simon Manschitz at Honda Research Institute Europe. I build assistive teleoperation and shared autonomy systems for dexterous manipulation: the robot takes care of safety and precision, and the human stays in charge of the task.

My recent work enforces safety in shared autonomy as a hard constraint with control barrier functions (BarrierIK, ICRA 2026) and brings assistance to the manipulation of ropes and cables (AssistDLO). Before my PhD, I worked on physical human–robot interaction at Koç University and studied control and automation engineering at Istanbul Technical University.

  • Assistive Teleoperation
  • Shared Autonomy
  • Control Barrier Functions
  • Deformable Linear Objects
  • Physical Human–Robot Interaction
  • Robot Learning
