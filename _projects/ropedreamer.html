--- title: "RopeDreamer: Kinematic Recurrent State Space Model for DLO Dynamics" pub: ropedreamer theme: dlo topic: DLO · Dynamics prediction order: 3 related: [forwarddlo] summary: >- A DLO dynamics-prediction model: a recurrent state-space model over a quaternion kinematic-chain rope representation keeps link lengths fixed by construction and stays stable and topologically consistent over long rollouts, including loops and crossings. ai_summary: >-

Learned rope models often predict well for a few steps and then drift: segments stretch, pass through each other, or lose loops. RopeDreamer keeps long rollouts physically plausible with two ideas. The rope is represented as a kinematic chain (the first segment's position plus relative rotations between consecutive links), so constant link length is built into the state. Dynamics are learned with a recurrent state-space model that rolls forward in a compact latent space, with separate decoders for reconstructing the current state and predicting the next.

On a million simulated pick-and-place transitions with self-crossings, it cuts 50-step open-loop error by 40.5% and per-step inference time by 31% compared to the best baseline, and preserves rope topology (measured with Gauss codes) far better. Its latent belief is the starting point of ForwardDLO.
