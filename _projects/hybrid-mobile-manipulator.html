For my B.Sc. thesis, supervised by Prof. Hakan Temeltaş, I built a mobile manipulator that combines a rigid, 3D-printed arm with a soft pneumatic gripper, and demonstrated it on autonomous navigation with SLAM, visual recognition of AR-tagged boxes, and pick-and-place of the objects on top of them.

My contributions

  • Designed a serial robotic manipulator in Siemens NX and manufactured it with 3D printing.
  • Designed a soft PneuNets gripper and analyzed it with finite element methods in Abaqus.
  • Integrated the manipulator with the mobile platform.
  • Developed the ROS packages for the manipulator in Python.
  • Tracked the marked boxes from ARTag fiducial markers with OpenCV.
  • Used SLAM from the ROS Navigation Stack to estimate the platform pose from LIDAR and IMU.
