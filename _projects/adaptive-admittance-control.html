--- title: "Adaptive Admittance Control via Deep-Learning Subtask Classification" pub: mechatronics theme: phri topic: pHRI · Subtask recognition order: 2 summary: >- A neural network classifies a collaborative drilling task into Idle, Driving and Contact phases in real time, and the admittance controller adapts to each phase: transparent while the operator drives the robot, stable in contact with a stiff workpiece. ai_summary: >-

In collaborative drilling, the same admittance parameters cannot be both light enough for moving the robot freely and stiff enough for stable contact with the workpiece. A neural network recognizes the current subtask (Idle, Driving, or Contact) from interaction data in real time, and the admittance controller switches its parameters accordingly.

The classifier detects the subtasks with 98% accuracy across 12 participants, and the adaptation lowers human effort while driving by 20% and oscillation amplitude while drilling by 25% compared to fixed parameters.

  • Developed the methods that adapt the admittance parameters from the classifier's output.
