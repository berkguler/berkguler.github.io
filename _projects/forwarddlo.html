--- title: "ForwardDLO: Model-Based Bimanual Shape Matching of Unconstrained DLOs" pub: forwarddlo theme: dlo topic: DLO · Dynamics prediction order: 2 related: [ropedreamer] summary: >- A DLO dynamics-prediction model: it forecasts how a rope lying freely on a table deforms under bimanual pick-and-place actions, and is fast enough to score about 9,000 candidate actions per 30 Hz planning step for model-predictive shape matching. ai_summary: >-

ForwardDLO predicts how an unconstrained rope deforms when two arms grasp it anywhere along its length. Because each arm chooses a grasp point, direction and distance, the action space is huge, so for model-predictive control the model's speed matters as much as its accuracy.

The model builds on the recurrent latent belief of RopeDreamer but decodes every rope segment with one shared, lightweight decoder and re-grounds each rollout step on an explicit rope shape. It has no explicit segment-to-segment operations, which makes batched evaluation of candidate actions cheap: on real rope data it has the lowest long-horizon error of the learned models (13% below the strongest baseline), matches them in real-world S/U/J shape matching on an ALOHA robot, and evaluates 8–22× more candidate actions within the same 30 Hz budget.
